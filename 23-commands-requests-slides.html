Reminder: the short command

  • Lesson 8: take off -х and you have a command. Ир! = Come!
  • The book calls it a direct command, to one person or to several.
  • Good friends and family use it often. It is the familiar way to ask.
  • Say it twice to make it polite: Суу суу!
Маргааш ир!
Come tomorrow!
Багшаас асуу!
Ask the teacher!
Та ор ор!
Do come in.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 177, 203

-аарай: I expect you will

  • The book calls this ending a demand. The speaker is quite sure it will be done.
  • So do not use it when the other person should be free to say no.
  • It is very common, to чи and to та. The book puts "please" in the English.
  • After a long vowel or ий, a г goes in: хий → хийгээрэй, уу → уугаарай. And ярь → яриарай.

-аач: please, I am asking

  • The book calls this ending a request: a concrete demand or an urgent request.
  • It is usually said to чи.
  • The book says -аарай is like it, only more demanding.
  • Same vowel pattern: яв → яваач, ир → ирээч, оч → очооч, өг → өгөөч.
  • Same extras: хий → хийгээч, ярь → яриач.
Чи манайд ирээч!
Please come to my home.
Надад туслаач!
Please help me!
Хараач!
Look!
Mongolian Grammar pp. 26, 28, 178–179, 403

-я, -ё, -е: let me, let's

  • This ending is about yourself: "let me ..." or "let's ...". The book calls it an intention or a wish.
  • With no би or бид in the sentence, it usually means "let's".
  • After a consonant, write ъ before -я and -ё, and ь before -е.
  • After a vowel or ь, write just the ending.

Don't: битгий

  • Put битгий in front of the verb.
  • It works with the short command, with -аарай and with -аач.
  • There is no "don't" for -я.
  • бүү does the same job. The book says it is stronger, and formal. Say битгий; know бүү when you see it.
Чи одоо битгий унт!
Don't sleep now!
Битгий бичээрэй!
Please don't write!
Чи битгий яваач.
Please do not go.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 175, 177–179, 181, 334

Side by side

  • Command: direct, for family and friends. -аарай: you expect a yes. -аач: an urgent request, mostly to чи. -я: let me, let's.
  • The book has six more endings of this kind (-сугай, -уузай, -гтун, -г, -аасай, -тугай). They can wait.

Your turn

  • One person gives short commands and the others do them: Бос! Суу! Ир! Яв! Унш! Бич!
  • Now add битгий. The others must not move: Битгий бос! Битгий суу! Битгий яв!
  • Ask for things on the table with өгөөч: Надад ном өгөөч. Надад талх өгөөч.
  • Take turns suggesting something with -я: Явъя! Зураг авъя! Хоол хийе! The others answer: За, тэгье!
  • Someone walks to the door. Everyone says: Сайн яваарай! Маргааш ирээрэй!
