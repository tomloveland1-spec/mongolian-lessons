Four vowels to practise: о, у, ө, ү

  • о: open, like "bought". у: close to the o in "bone".
  • ө: like the oo in "good". ү: like the oo in "stoop".
  • The book lists these four among the hard sounds. Mixing them up changes the word.
ус / үс
water / hair
ор / өр
bed / debt
уул / үүл
mountain / cloud
Mongolian Grammar p. 19

Long vowels: write the letter twice

  • A double letter means: hold the sound longer. аа, оо, уу, ээ, өө, үү.
  • Long и is written ий.
  • Short or long makes a different word, so listen carefully.
  • Do not drag them out. Mongolian long vowels are shorter than English long vowels.
цас / цаас
snow / paper
хол / хоол
far / food
үд / үүд
noon / door
Mongolian Grammar p. 20

The y-vowels: я, е, ё, ю

  • Each one is a y sound plus a basic vowel, written as one letter.
  • я = Y + а ё = Y + о е = Y + э
  • ю does two jobs: Y + у or Y + ү.
  • They can be long too: юу (what).

й and ы

  • й is called "half и" (хагас и). It never stands alone. It follows a vowel and blends into it: ай, ой, уй, эй, үй. Lesson 5 practises these.
  • ы sounds like a long ee. It appears only in endings.
  • ы goes on back-vowel words. Front-vowel words use ий instead.

One sound changes the word: write the missing vowel (one letter or two)

Snow is цас. Paper has the long vowel: цаас
Food is хоол. Far has the short vowel: хол
Noon is үд. Door has the long vowel: үүд
Cloud is үүл. Mountain is the back-vowel twin: уул
Hair is үс. Water is the back-vowel twin: ус

Take the y-vowel apart

я = Y + а
ё = Y + о
е = Y + э
ю = Y + у or Y + ү

Your turn

  • Warm-up: greet each other. Сайн байна уу? (Hello: sain bain uu). Answer: Сайн. Сайн байна уу?
  • One person says ус or үс, ор or өр. Everyone else points to the one they heard.
  • Say each pair, short then long: цас, цаас. хол, хоол. үд, үүд.
  • Read the y-vowel words aloud: яс, ёс, юм, юу, ер.
  • Say таны, then миний. Which one is the back word?
