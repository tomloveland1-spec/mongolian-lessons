"From" is an ending: -аас

  • English puts "from" in front of a word. Mongolian sticks an ending on the back of it.
  • The ending has four shapes. Look at the vowels in the word to pick one.
  • а, у words take -аас. о words take -оос.
  • э, ү words take -ээс. ө words take -өөс: хөл (foot) becomes хөлөөс.
ах → ахаас
older brother → from older brother
ээж → ээжээс
mother → from mother
ном → номоос
book → from the book
Mongolian Grammar p. 90

Watch out: an extra letter

  • Some words slip in an н before the ending: ус → уснаас (from the water), цай → цайнаас, талх → талхнаас.
  • Words that end in a long vowel add г first: дүү → дүүгээс.
  • A final ь turns into и: сургууль → сургуулиас (from school).
  • You do not have to guess these. Learn them one at a time as you meet them.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 90, 91

From me, from you

  • би, чи, та and тэр change shape before the ending: над-, чам-, тан-, түүн-.
  • You may also hear бид нараас and тэд нараас.
  • To ask "from whom?" say хэнээс. To ask "from where?" say хаанаас.

Ask from someone

  • In English you ask someone. In Mongolian you ask FROM someone.
  • So the person you ask always gets the "from" ending.
  • битгий before the verb means "don't".
Түүнээс асуу!
Ask him! / Ask her!
Тэднээс битгий асуу!
Don't ask them!
Би багшаас асууна.
I will ask the teacher.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 259, 260

Bigger than me

  • To compare, put the "from" ending on the person or thing you compare with.
  • There is no word for "than" and no ending like English "-er".
  • Ах надаас том is, word for word, "Older brother from-me big".
Та надаас настай.
You are older than I. (настай = old)
Энэ хүн тэр хүнээс өндөр.
This person is taller than that person.
Ах надаас том.
My older brother is bigger than me.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 92, 215

Conversation

АЧи хаанаас ирсэн бэ?
Where did you come from?
ББи дэлгүүрээс ирсэн.
I came from the shop.
АЭнэ номыг хэнээс авсан бэ?
Who did you get this book from?
ББи энэ номыг багшаас авсан.
I got this book from the teacher.
ААх чамаас том уу?
Is your older brother bigger than you?
БТийм, ах надаас том. Би дүүгээс том.
Yes, my older brother is bigger than me. I am bigger than my younger sibling.

Your turn

  • Point at someone and tell the person next to you: Түүнээс асуу!
  • Compare two people in the room: … надаас өндөр. / Би …-аас том.
  • Ask: Чи хаанаас ирсэн бэ? Answer with a place: Би дэлгүүрээс ирсэн. / Би хотоос ирсэн.
  • Say all seven "from" words in order without looking: надаас, чамаас, танаас …
  • Act out the conversation, then swap parts.
