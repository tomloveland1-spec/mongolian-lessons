Lesson 15

Надаар · By bus, with a pencil (instrumental case)

Нэр / Name: ____________________

Say what you do something with: by bus, with a pencil, in Mongolian. Learn the seven "by me" words and what they are really used for.

3. Add the ending: -аар, -ээр, -оор or -өөр?

  1. гар ___ (by hand)
  2. машин ___ (by car)
  3. онгоц ___ (by plane)
  4. хэл ___ (in a language)
  5. бөмбөг ___ (with a ball)
  6. автобус ___ (by bus)
  7. монгол ___ (in Mongolian)
  8. ээж ___ (through mother)
  9. хот ___ (through the city)
  10. харандаа ___ (with a pencil; long vowel!)

4. Which "by" word fits?

  1. Тэр ___ туслуулсан. (He had me help him.)
  2. Тэр ___ туслуулсан. (He had us help him.)
  3. Чи ___ бичүүл. (Have them write it.)
  4. Ээж ___ захиа явуулсан уу? (Did mother send the letter with you? To a friend.)

5. Write it in Mongolian

  1. by bus
  2. I will go by car.
  3. I write with a pencil.
  4. Speak in Mongolian!
  5. I will play with a ball.
