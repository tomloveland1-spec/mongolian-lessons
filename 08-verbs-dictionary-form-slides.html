Dictionary verbs end in -х

  • Word lists and many dictionaries give every verb with -х. It is like the English "to ...": явах = to go.
  • After a consonant there is a short helper vowel before the -х: яв + а + х, ир + э + х.
  • You cannot finish a plain sentence with the -х form. First we take the -х off, then we add a different ending.
явах
to go
хэлэх
to say
уух
to drink
Mongolian Grammar p. 147

Take off -х and you get a command

  • Take off -х and the one short vowel in front of it: явах → яв, ирэх → ир, идэх → ид, унших → унш.
  • A double vowel or ий stays: уух → уу, суух → суу, асуух → асуу, хийх → хий.
  • This short form is called the stem. Said by itself it is a command for family and friends: Ир! = Come!
  • Say it twice to make it more polite.
Маргааш ир!
Come tomorrow!
Та ярь!
Speak!
Чи суу суу!
Do sit down.
Mongolian Grammar p. 177

Four stems to watch

  • ярих → ярь! (the и turns into ь)
  • туслах → тусал! (л and а swap places)
  • ажиллах → ажилла! (the а stays)
  • тоглох → тогло! (the о stays)
  • Every other verb in this lesson follows the normal rule.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 25–27, 177

The verb comes last

  • In English the verb is in the middle: I read a book.
  • In Mongolian the verb is always the last word: I + book + read.
  • Who does it comes first. The thing you eat, drink or read goes just before the verb.
  • When you mean "a" or "some", that noun needs no ending at all.
Би ном уншина.
I will read a book. (I + book + will read)
Ээж цай ууна.
Mother will drink tea.
Би маргааш явна.
I will go tomorrow. (I + tomorrow + will go)
Mongolian Grammar pp. 87, 191, 382–383

Will do: -на, -нэ, -но, -нө

  • Stem + this ending = "will do" (or "does"). It is the same for I, you, he, she, we and they.
  • Look at the first vowel of the verb. а, у, я → -на. э, и, ү → -нэ. о → -но. ө → -нө.
  • A few verbs keep a helper vowel before it: уншина, сонсоно, унтана.
  • For a question, use the -х form and finish with вэ / бэ or уу / үү: Чи юу хийх вэ?
яв → явна, ир → ирнэ
will go, will come
оч → очно, өг → өгнө
will go there, will give
Чи юу хийх вэ?
What will you do?
Mongolian Grammar pp. 184, 191 (questions: p. 147)

Your turn

  • Simon says: one person gives a command (Бос! Суу! Яв! Ир! Унш! Бич! Унт!) and everyone acts it out.
  • Pick a verb card, say the dictionary form, then the command: унтах → Унт!
  • Ask each other Чи юу хийх вэ? and answer with Би ...на / ...нэ / ...но / ...нө.
  • Mime a verb. The others shout the Mongolian word.
  • One person says a food or drink (цай, сүү, талх, мах, ус). The next person makes a sentence: Би цай ууна. Би талх иднэ.
