Whose? The ending -ын / -ийн

  • Any person or thing can be an owner. Add an ending that works like the English 's.
  • The owner goes in front of the thing that is owned. The owned word does not change.
  • Words with а, о, у take -ын: аав → аавын, ах → ахын, найз → найзын, ном → номын.
  • Words with э, ө, ү take -ийн: гэр → гэрийн.
  • After ж, ч, ш it is always -ийн: ээж → ээжийн, эгч → эгчийн, багш → багшийн, Дорж → Доржийн.
Энэ хэний ээж вэ?
Whose mother is this?
Энэ Доржийн ээж.
This is Dorj's mother.
Энэ аавын ном.
This is Father's book.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 79–80

A few owners look different

  • Words that end in н only add a vowel: хүн → хүний (the person's), хэн → хэний (whose).
  • Some words bring back a hidden н: морь → морины (the horse's), ус → усны (of water), талх → талхны (of bread).
  • Words that end in ай, ой, ий just add -н: далай → далайн (of the sea).
  • You do not need to learn these all now. Just notice them when you meet them.
номын хавтас
the book's cover
хүний нэр
a person's name
Mongolian Grammar pp. 80–81

My, your, his: seven words to learn

  • The pronouns change shape, so learn these as whole words.
  • They go in front of the owned thing: миний ном, миний аав, миний нэр.

Манай and танай

  • When "my" does not really fit, because the person or thing is not yours alone, Mongolians say манай. It sounds modest: "not mine alone".
  • So манай ээж is the normal way to say "my mother".
  • Миний puts a strong stress on MY.
  • Танай is also used for "your". The book says it means the same as таны: only "my" needs the modesty.
манай хүүхдүүд
my children (the normal, modest way)
миний хүүхдүүд
MY children
Танай хүүхэд сайн уу?
Is your child well?
Mongolian Grammar pp. 104, 258

Your turn

  • Point at someone's things and say whose they are: Энэ аавын ном. Энэ ээжийн цай.
  • Hold up something and ask: Энэ хэний ном бэ? The owner answers: Энэ миний ном.
  • Go round the room. Each person says: Миний нэр …
  • Ask a grown-up Таны нэр хэн бэ? and ask a child Чиний нэр хэн бэ?
  • Tell everyone about your family with манай: Манай аав … Манай ээж …
