  3. You are 10. Your sister is 6. She is your ___ .
  4. You are 10. Your brother is 3. He is your ___ .

4. Put the words in the right order

  1. шинэ / энэ / ном (This is a new book.)
  2. ном / шинэ / энэ (This book is new.)
  3. том / нохой / тэр (That dog is big.)
  4. багш / сайн / тэр (He is a good teacher.)
  5. биш / гэр / том / энэ (This home is not big.)

5. Make it plural

  2. гэр → ___ (homes)
  3. багш → ___ (teachers)
  4. ах → ___ (older brothers)
  5. дүү → ___ (younger brothers and sisters)
