Эрт урьд цагт · Three more pasts: -лаа, -в, -жээ

Нэр / Name: ____________________

Know the three other past endings: -лаа for what just happened, -в in writing, and -жээ for stories and things you did not see. Tell a short story.

5. Everyday past (-сан, -сэн) or story past (-жээ)?

  1. Би цай уу___. (I did it myself)
  2. Эрт урьд цагт нэг морь бай___. (the start of a story)
  3. Чи хот яв___ уу? (a question)
  4. Бид өчигдөр мах ид___. (we did it ourselves)
  5. Орой бол___. (it happened by itself; nobody did it)
