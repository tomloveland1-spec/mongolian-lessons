Lesson 17

Над руу · Towards, and all eight cases (directive case)

Нэр / Name: ____________________

Say where someone is heading with руу, рүү, луу or лүү, then line up all eight cases on one noun and one pronoun.

Words

2.утасдахB.towards them

5.тан рууE.towards whom

3. руу, рүү, луу or лүү?

  1. хот ___ (to the city)
  2. ээж ___ (to mother)
  3. гэр ___ (home)
  4. сар ___ (to the moon)
  5. сургууль ___ (to school)
  6. дэлгүүр ___ (to the shop)
  7. багш ___ (to the teacher)
  8. эгч ___ (to older sister)
  9. та нар ___ (to all of you)
  10. хүн ___ (towards a person)

4. Which "towards" word fits?

  1. ___ утасдаарай. (Please phone me.)
  2. Би ___ очно. (I will come to you. To a friend.)
  3. ___ оч! (Go to him!)
  4. Нохой ___ гүйнэ. (The dog runs towards us.)
  5. Би ___ очно. (I will go to them.)

5. Review: put аав in the right case

  1. Энэ ___ ном. (This is Father's book.)
  2. Би ___ хүлээнэ. (I will wait for Father.)
  3. Би ___ цай өгнө. (I will give Father tea.)
  4. Би ___ асууна. (I will ask Father.)
  5. Би ___ явна. (I will go with Father.)
  6. Би ___ очно. (I will go to Father.)
