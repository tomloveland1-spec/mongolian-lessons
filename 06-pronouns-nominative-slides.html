Seven pronouns

  • One person: би (I), чи or та (you), тэр (he, she, it).
  • More than one: бид (we), та нар (you all), тэд (they).
  • тэр means he, she and it. Mongolian does not tell them apart.
  • These are the forms for the one who does the action. Other forms (my, me, to me) come later.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 256–257

Чи or та?

  • чи: for people younger than you, or close friends about your own age.
  • та: for people older than you. It is the polite "you".
  • тэр is really the word for "that", so it is not very polite for a person. Use the name when you can.
Чи хэн бэ?
Who are you? (to a child or friend)
Та хэн бэ?
Who are you? (polite)
Mongolian Grammar pp. 256, 259

No word for "is"

  • To say what something is, put the two words side by side. A plain present sentence normally has no "am / is / are".
  • Add биш after the noun to say "is not".
Би багш.
I am a teacher.
Энэ ном.
This is a book.
Тэр эмч биш.
He / she is not a doctor.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 194, 328–329

We, you all, they

  • та used to mean "you all". Now it is the polite word for one person, so for several people say та нар.
  • You may also hear бид нар and тэд нар. Adding нар stresses that it is a whole group.
  • The noun stays the same. Mongolian leaves out the plural when it is already clear.
Бид оюутан.
We are students.
Тэд багш.
They are teachers.
Та нар эмч үү?
Are you (all) doctors?
Mongolian Grammar pp. 72, 256

Questions with a question word: бэ / вэ

  • If the sentence has a question word (хэн, юу …), finish it with бэ or вэ.
  • After м, н, в → бэ
  • After any other letter → вэ
Та хэн бэ?
Who are you?
Энэ юу вэ?
What is this?
Mongolian Grammar p. 327

Yes/no questions: уу / үү / юу / юү

  • If there is no question word, finish the sentence with this particle instead.
  • After a long vowel or diphthong → юу / юү
  • After anything else → уу / үү
  • Pick у or ү to match the vowels of the word before it: а, о, у words take уу; э, ө, ү words take үү. Words with only и take үү too.
Бат оюутан уу?
Is Bat a student?
Бат эмч үү?
Is Bat a doctor?
Энэ харандаа юу?
Is this a pencil?
Mongolian Grammar pp. 21, 328

Conversation

АСайн байна уу?
Hello.
БСайн. Сайн байна уу?
Fine. Hello.
АТа хэн бэ?
Who are you?
ББи Бат. Би багш. Та нар оюутан уу?
I'm Bat. I'm a teacher. Are you (all) students?
АТийм, бид оюутан.
Yes, we are students.
БТэд хэн бэ?
Who are they?
АТэд эмч.
They are doctors.

Your turn

  • Point and say the pronoun: at yourself (би), a parent (та), a brother or sister (чи), everyone together (бид), the others (та нар).
  • Point at someone in the room and ask: Тэр хэн бэ? Point at two people and ask: Тэд хэн бэ?
  • Hold up an object and ask: Энэ юу вэ?
  • Ask a yes/no question that should get the answer Үгүй, … биш.
  • Act out the conversation with your own names.
