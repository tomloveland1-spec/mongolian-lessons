One to ten

  • Every number has a counting form. Use it when you count: нэг, хоёр, гурав …
  • Most numbers also have a second form that ends in н. It is used before a noun.
  • нэг and хоёр stay the same before a noun.

Building bigger numbers

  • Say the tens first, then the ones: арван нэг (11), хорин хоёр (22).
  • Every word takes its н form except the last one.
  • Hundreds work the same way: гурван зуун арав (310).
  • хоёр and мянга never add н inside a number, and neither does нэг before зуун: хоёр зуун хоёр (202), хоёр мянга (2,000).
гучин ес

A number before a noun

  • Before a noun the number takes its н form: гурав → гурван ном.
  • нэг and хоёр do not change: нэг ном, хоёр ном.
  • When you just count, or say a clock time without цаг, there is no н. (Dates do take the н form.)
  • The noun stays as it is. There is no plural ending after a number.
дөрвөн хүн
four people
гурван ном
three books
Баатар хоёр ном авсан.
Baatar bought two books.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 236–238

How many? How old?

  • хэд asks "how many". Before a noun it is хэдэн, just like a number.
  • To say your age: Би + number + настай. The number takes its н form: далан настай (70 years old).
  • In 11, 21, 31 … the нэг becomes нэгэн before a noun: арван нэгэн настай (11 years old).
  • настай is нас (age) with the ending -тай (with). Learn it as one word. Lesson 16 explains -тай.
Чи хэдэн настай вэ?
How old are you?
Би арван гурван настай.
I am thirteen years old.
Энэ хэд вэ?
How much is this?
Mongolian Grammar pp. 236, 272

What time is it?

  • With цаг (o'clock) the number takes its н form. If you leave цаг out, the number has no н.
  • хагас after the number means "half past".
  • болж байна means "it is becoming". Learn it as a fixed phrase. Lesson 19 explains it.
  • Би таван цагт ирнэ. = I'll come at 5 o'clock. Lesson 13 explains the -т.
Одоо хэдэн цаг болж байна вэ?
What time is it now?
Дөрвөн цаг болж байна. / Дөрөв болж байна.
It's four o'clock. / It's four.
Одоо арав хагас болж байна.
It's half past ten.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 84, 238

Your turn

  • Count round the room from нэг to арав, one number each. Then count back down from арав.
  • Stack up books one at a time and count them: нэг ном, хоёр ном, гурван ном, дөрвөн ном …
  • Ask everyone their age. To a child: Чи хэдэн настай вэ? To a grown-up: Та хэдэн настай вэ? Answer: Би … настай.
  • One person writes a number from 11 to 99. The others race to say it: хорин тав!
  • Look at a clock. Ask Одоо хэдэн цаг болж байна вэ? and answer … цаг болж байна.
