# Mongolian lessons

**Open the lessons: https://tomloveland1-spec.github.io/mongolian-lessons/**

Slides and printable worksheets for a 25-lesson beginner course in Mongolian, with the teaching schedule below. Rules and many examples are quoted from R. Kullmann and D. Tserenpil, *Mongolian Grammar* (4th rev. ed., 2008); dialogues and most exercise sentences were composed to follow its patterns and have not been checked by a native speaker.


Three sessions a week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday), starting Monday, November 16, 2026 and ending Friday, May 21, 2027. Two weeks off over Christmas.

## A normal week (lesson 6 onward)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Last week's words as a warm-up, then the new words and the grammar slides |
| Wednesday | The conversation and the speaking activities; hand out the worksheet |
| Friday | Go over the worksheet, then a flash-card quiz on the week's words |

Lessons 1–5 take one session each.
