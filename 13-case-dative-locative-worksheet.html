Lesson 13

Надад ном байна · To Mother, at school, I have (dative-locative case)

Нэр / Name: ____________________

Use the ending -д / -т for the person who gets something and for the place where something is, say "to me, to you", and say what you have.

3. Add the ending: -д or -т?

  1. Аав гэр___ байна. (Father is in the ger.)
  2. Багш сургууль___ байна. (The teacher is at school.)
  3. Ээж дэлгүүр___ байна. (Mother is at the shop.)
  4. Би аав___ ном өгнө. (I will give Father a book.)
  5. Би дүү___ талх өгнө. (I will give my little brother bread.)

4. Change the word in brackets to fit the gap

  1. ___ ном байна. (би)
  2. ___ талх байна уу? (чи)
  3. Би ___ цай өгнө. (та)
  4. Багш ___ ном өгнө. (тэр)
  5. ___ сүү байхгүй. (бид)
  6. Би ___ тусална. (та нар)
  7. Бид ___ хоол өгнө. (тэд)

5. Write it in Mongolian (use байна for "have")

  1. I have a book.
  2. Do you have any water? (to a friend)
  3. We do not have any bread.
  4. Please give me some tea.
  5. Mother is at home. (at home = гэртээ)
  6. I will give you bread. (to an older person)
