"Towards" is a little word: руу

  • руу means to or towards. It comes after the word, not before it.
  • It is written as a separate word, with a space.
  • а, о, у words take руу.
  • э, ө, ү words take рүү.
хот руу
to the city
сургууль руу
to school
ээж рүү
to mother
Mongolian Grammar p. 99

After р, say луу or лүү

  • After a word that ends in р, the р of руу changes to л.
  • а, о, у words ending in р take луу.
  • э, ө, ү words ending in р take лүү.
сар луу
to the moon
гэр лүү
to the home, to the yurt
Баатар луу
to Baatar
Mongolian Grammar p. 99

Towards me, towards you

  • Use the same changed shapes as in Lessons 13 to 16: над, чам, тан, түүн. Then add руу or рүү.
  • бид and тэд become бидэн рүү and тэдэн рүү.
  • та нар ends in р, so it is та нар луу.
  • You may also hear бид нар луу and тэд нар луу.
Би чам руу очно.
I will come to you.
Түүн рүү оч!
Go to him! / Go to her!
Над руу утасдаарай.
Please phone me. (Lesson 23 explains -аарай.)
Mongolian Grammar pp. 99, 256 to 258

When do you use it?

  • With going, coming and running: the place or person you are heading for.
  • With looking: the thing you look at.
  • With phoning and sending: the person it goes to.
  • To ask "where to?" say хаашаа. To ask "to whom?" say хэн рүү.
Та Москва руу хэзээ явах вэ?
When are you leaving for Moscow?
Тэр над руу харсан.
He looked towards me.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 99, 260

All eight cases on one word

  • Every case is an ending (or руу) on the same word. The plain word with no ending is a case too.
  • A regular noun just adds the ending. би changes shape, which is why its forms must be learned.

Your turn

  • Ask each person: Чи хаашаа явах вэ? They answer: Би … руу явна.
  • Point at someone and give an order: Түүн рүү оч! Then: Над руу ир!
  • Say a word that ends in р and let the others call out луу or лүү: гэр, сар, дэлгүүр, та нар.
  • Pick a person word (ээж, ах, багш) and say it in all eight cases, like the table.
  • Act out the conversation, then swap parts.
