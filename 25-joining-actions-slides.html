Only the last verb tells the time

  • English: "I went to the shop and bought bread." Both verbs are past.
  • Mongolian puts the past, present or future ending on the last verb only.
  • Every verb before it takes a joining ending instead: -ж or -аад.
  • The joining endings are the same for I, you, he, she, we and they.
Би дэлгүүр явсан. Би талх авсан.
I went to the shop. I bought bread.
Би дэлгүүр явж талх авсан.
I went to the shop and bought bread.
Би дэлгүүр явж талх авна.
I will go to the shop and buy bread.
Mongolian Grammar p. 156

-ж: and

  • Use -ж for two actions side by side: at the same time, or simply one after the other.
  • Make it just as in lesson 19: хийх → хийж, уух → ууж, очих → очиж, явах → явж.
  • Remember the few verbs with -ч: авах → авч, өгөх → өгч, сурах → сурч.
  • You know this ending already. бичиж байна is бичиж joined to байна.
Ээж хоол хийж, аав цай ууж байна.
Mother is cooking, Father is drinking tea.
Би сургуульд очиж ном уншсан.
I went to school and read a book.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 28–29, 157

-аад: and then

  • Use -аад when the first action is finished before the next one starts.
  • Think "and then", or "after": after I got up, I drank tea.
  • You can line up several -аад verbs in a row.
  • тэгээд starts a new sentence with "And then ...".
Би өглөө босоод цайгаа уугаад ажилдаа явсан.
I got up in the morning, had my tea, and then went to work.
Тэр энд ирээд ном аваад явсан.
He came here, took a book and left. (энд = here)
Би дэлгүүр явсан. Тэгээд талх авсан.
I went to the shop. And then I bought bread.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 159–160, 321, 348

Four shapes: -аад, -ээд, -оод, -өөд

  • Pick the vowel just as you did for -аагүй in lesson 9.
  • а or у → -аад. э, ү or и → -ээд. о → -оод. ө → -өөд: төгсөх (to graduate) → төгсөөд.
  • After a long vowel or ий, put г in first: уух → уугаад, хийх → хийгээд.
  • ярих → яриад.

-ж or -аад?

  • -ж: the actions go side by side. They are not tightly linked.
  • -аад: first one, then the other. The second follows from the first.
  • Two people doing things at the same time: use -ж.
  • One sentence can use both.
Би цай ууж ном уншсан.
I drank tea and read a book.
Би цай уугаад ном уншсан.
I drank tea, and then I read a book.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 157, 159–160

I got up in the morning and then drank tea. After that I went to school.

Your turn

  • Say two things you did this morning, in order, in one sentence: Би босоод цай уусан.
  • Look round the room and say what two people are doing at the same time: Ээж ном уншиж, аав цай ууж байна.
  • Chain game. One person starts: Би босоод ... The next person repeats it and adds one more -аад action before the last verb.
  • Say one sentence three ways by changing only the last word: Би цай уугаад ном уншсан. / уншина. / уншдаг.
  • Read the long sentence from the last slide aloud together. Then swap one piece: автобусаар → машинаар, хот руу → сургууль руу.
