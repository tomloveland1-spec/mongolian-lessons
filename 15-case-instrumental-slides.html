"By" and "with" are an ending: -аар

  • This ending tells you what is used to do something. English says by, with, in or through.
  • It has four shapes, chosen by the vowels in the word, just like -аас.
  • а, у words take -аар. о words take -оор.
  • э, ү words take -ээр. ө words take -өөр: мөнгө (money) becomes мөнгөөр.
гар → гараар
hand → by hand
онгоц → онгоцоор
plane → by plane
хэл → хэлээр
language → in a language
Mongolian Grammar pp. 93, 94

Tools and rides

  • Use it for the thing you ride: by bus, by car, by train.
  • Use it for the tool or stuff you use: with a pencil, with water.
  • Words that end in a long vowel or in ай, ой, уй add г first: харандаа → харандаагаар, дугуй (bicycle) → дугуйгаар.
Тэр галт тэргээр явна.
She will go by train.
Үүнийг халуун усаар угаа!
Wash this with hot water!
Би харандаагаар бичнэ.
I write with a pencil.
Mongolian Grammar pp. 93, 94

Roads and languages

  • Use it for the way you go: along the street, up the stairs, through the forest.
  • Use it for the language you speak. No word for "in" is needed.
  • монгол → монголоор means "in Mongolian".
Шатаар гар!
Go up the stairs!
Монголоор ярь!
Speak in Mongolian!
Та утсаар ярих уу?
Will you talk on the phone? (утас = phone)
Mongolian Grammar pp. 93–96

By me, by you: what are they for?

  • The same changed shapes as in Lesson 14 (над-, чам-, тан-), with -аар.
  • "By him / her" has two forms. The book calls түүнээр the correct one and says түүгээр is widely used in everyday speech.
  • Use these words when you get a person to do a job for you, or send something with a person.
  • The verbs here have a special "get someone to do it" shape. For now, learn the sentences whole.

Conversation

АЧи сургуульд юугаар явах вэ?
How will you go to school? (By what?)
ББи автобусаар явна. Чи юугаар явах вэ?
I will go by bus. How will you go?
АБи машинаар явна.
I will go by car.
БЧи монголоор ярих уу?
Will you speak Mongolian?
АТийм, би монголоор ярина.
Yes, I will speak Mongolian.

Your turn

  • Ask each person: Чи сургуульд юугаар явах вэ? They answer: Би …-аар явна.
  • Hold up a pencil and say: Би харандаагаар бичнэ.
  • Tell someone: Монголоор ярь! (Speak in Mongolian!)
  • Say all seven "by" words in order without looking: надаар, чамаар, танаар …
  • Act out the conversation, then swap parts.
