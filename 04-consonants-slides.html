20 consonant letters

  • Of the 35 letters, 20 are consonants.
  • М, Т and К look and sound much like English.
  • Many have a new shape but an easy sound: б, д, ж, п, ф, ч, ш.
  • A few look like English letters but make a different sound. Those are the traps.

талх

Mongolian Grammar p. 19

Look-alikes: do not trust your eyes

  • В в is v. Н н is n. Р р is r. С с is s.
  • Х х is kh, not x.
  • У у is not y. It is a vowel.
  • Read the three words below. None of them says what an English reader expects.
нар
says "nar" (sun)
сар
says "sar" (moon, month)
хар
says "khar" (black)
Mongolian Grammar p. 19

Tricky sounds: в, г, з

  • в: like v in "vase". Right before б it is said as б, so авбал sounds like аббал.
  • г: like g in "get" at the start of a word. In the middle or at the end it can sound harder. The book compares бага to English "back".
  • з: not the English z. Say dz, like the end of "seeds".

No English match: л, р, х

  • The book says these three must be practised well.
  • л: put your tongue against your top teeth, not behind them as in English.
  • р: always rolled, like a Spanish or Scottish r.
  • х: breathy, from the back of the mouth, like the end of Scottish "loch".

н at the end of a word, and ц

  • н at the start of a word is a plain n, as in "name".
  • н at the end of a word sounds like ng in "sing".
  • ц is ts, said as one quick sound, as in "its". It can start a word.

Letters for borrowed words

  • к, ф and щ appear only in words borrowed from other languages.
  • True Mongolian words never begin with л or п, so a word that starts with п is borrowed too.
  • Borrowed words are often easy to read: sound them out and you may hear the English word.

парк

Mongolian Grammar pp. 19, 21

Seven consonants that need a vowel

  • м, н, г, л, б, в, р always have a vowel right before or right after them.
  • Memory trick: they are the consonants in монгол баавар.
  • The other nine, д, ж, з, с, т, х, ц, ч, ш, can stand without a vowel. That is why words can end in two consonants.

Coming up in Lesson 5

  • й after a vowel makes one blended sound: ай, ой, уй, эй, үй.
  • The soft sign ь makes the consonant before it soft: морь (horse).
  • The hard sign ъ appears only in "let's …" forms: явъя (let's go).
Mongolian Grammar pp. 21–22

Your turn

  • Warm-up: greet each other. Сайн байна уу? (Hello: sain bain uu). Answer: Сайн. Сайн байна уу?
  • Read the look-alike row aloud: В, Н, Р, С, Х. Then read нар, сар, хар.
  • Roll your р: орох, барс, зураг, самар.
  • Say ном, then авсан. Listen for n at the start and ng at the end.
  • Sound out the borrowed words and guess them: парк, кило, фото, радио.
