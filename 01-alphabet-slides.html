Lesson 1

Цагаан толгой

The alphabet
Recognise all 35 letters of the Mongolian Cyrillic alphabet. Learn the name of each letter and the sound it makes.

Letters 1 of 3

А а
name: а
a as in father, but short
ах (older brother)
Б б
name: бэ
b as in bird
барс (tiger)
В в
name: вэ
v as in vase
авах (to take)
Г г
name: гэ
g as in get
гал (fire)
Д д
name: дэ
d as in day
дээвэр (roof)
Е е
name: е

ер (ninety)
Ё ё

yo as in yawn
ёс (custom, rule)
Ж ж
name: жэ

жимс (fruit)

name: зэ

зураг (picture)

name: и

name: ка
k as in kilo

Your turn

  • Warm-up: greet each other. Сайн байна уу? (Hello: sain bain uu). Answer: Сайн. Сайн байна уу?
  • Take turns picking a letter card. Say its name and its sound, then click to check.
  • Go around the table through the alphabet in order. Each person says the next letter's name.
  • One person says a sound. Everyone else points to the letter that makes it.
  • Click a card to hide it again, and see who still remembers its name.
