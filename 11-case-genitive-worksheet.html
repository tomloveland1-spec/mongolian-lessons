Lesson 11

Энэ хэний ном бэ? · Whose is it? (genitive case)

Нэр / Name: ____________________

Say whose something is with the ending -ын / -ийн, and use the words for my, your, his, our and their.

3. Add the ending: -ын or -ийн?

  1. аав___ ном (Father's book)
  2. багш___ нэр (the teacher's name)
  3. найз___ гэр (a friend's home)
  4. ээж___ цай (Mother's tea)
  5. ах___ найз (older brother's friend)

4. Change the word in brackets into its "whose" form

  1. ___ нэр Бат. (би)
  2. ___ нэр хэн бэ? (та)
  3. Энэ ___ ном уу? (чи)
  4. ___ аав багш. (тэд)
  5. ___ ээж эмч. (тэр)
  6. Энэ ___ гэр. (бид)
  7. ___ багш хэн бэ? (та нар)

5. Write it in Mongolian

  1. My name is Bat.
  2. What is your name? (to an older person)
  3. This is my book.
  4. Whose book is this?
  5. Their father is a teacher.
  6. This is Father's book.
